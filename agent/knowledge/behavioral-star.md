# Behavioral Interviews (STAR)

## The STAR method
Structure every behavioral answer as Situation, Task, Action, Result. Spend about 10 percent on situation and task, 60 percent on action, and 30 percent on the result. Use "I" rather than "we" so the interviewer can see your personal contribution. End with a measurable result and what you learned.

## Common questions
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate or manager.
- Describe a project that failed or missed its deadline. What did you learn?
- Tell me about the most technically challenging problem you solved.
- Describe a time you had to influence without authority.
- Tell me about a time you received critical feedback.

## What strong answers show
Ownership, clear communication, handling ambiguity, data-driven decisions, and growth. Weak answers are vague, blame others, skip the result, or have no numbers.
